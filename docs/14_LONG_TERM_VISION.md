# Lumina Long-Term Product Vision

**Last updated:** August 29, 2026

This document describes a strategic hypothesis, not a feature commitment. The
current source of truth for shipped capabilities remains `00_PROJECT_STATUS.md`.
The roadmap determines what Lumina is actively validating.

## Starting Position

Lumina begins as a beauty discovery and trust marketplace. It helps clients
discover and compare professionals, send requests, communicate, complete a
service, and leave completion-linked verified reviews.

The long-term opportunity is broader: become a quiet trust and clarity layer for
the beauty-service relationship without prematurely replacing the professional's
existing booking or salon-management tools.

## The Problem Beyond Discovery

Beauty-service information is often fragmented:

- Consultation instructions are verbal or scattered across messages.
- Inspiration and before-and-after photos live in different places.
- The client and professional may remember expectations differently.
- Service history and aftercare are difficult to find later.
- Disputes lack a clear, shared timeline.

Lumina can create value by organizing this real-world activity into an
understandable record while keeping the experience calm and simple.

## Guiding Principle

**Create clarity before conflict.**

For clients, that may eventually mean clearer expectations, service history,
photos, aftercare, and agreed terms.

For professionals, it may mean more consistent consultations, organized service
documentation, before-and-after records, and fewer “you said / I said” moments.

## Staged Evolution

### Now — Discovery and Trust

- Professional discovery and comparison
- Profiles, services, pricing, availability, and portfolio photos
- Requests, proposals, communication, and booking handoff
- Completed services and verified client reviews

### Next — Stronger Reputation and Easier Management

- Profile-completeness guidance
- Clear professional dashboard versus public-profile roles
- Easier portfolio and result management
- Data-backed completed-service, review, and response signals
- Better booking handoff and beta reporting

### Later — Service Clarity

- Structured consultations and agreed expectations
- Service records
- Before-and-after documentation
- Client consent and confirmation
- Policies and aftercare

### Much Later — Relationship Intelligence

- Client beauty history
- Deeper professional business tools where demand is proven
- Structured service recovery and dispute context
- Intelligent assistance based on real preferences, services, results, and feedback

## Results and Evidence Model

Not every uploaded image is verified. Lumina should use precise levels:

1. **Professional-submitted result** — added by the professional.
2. **Completed-service result** — linked to a completed Lumina service.
3. **Client-confirmed result** — linked to a completed service and confirmed by
   the client with appropriate consent.

A small V1 may allow a professional to choose **Single Photo** or **Before &
After**, select a service, add optional context, and publish it. That tests whether
structured proof of work improves trust without claiming more than Lumina knows.

## Beauty Intent and Outcome Intelligence

Over time, Lumina may learn from consented, reliable signals such as:

- Saved styles and viewed results
- Searches and requested services
- Consultation preferences
- Completed services
- Before-and-after results
- Feedback and repeat behavior

The useful loop is:

**Inspiration → Consultation → Agreement → Service → Result → Feedback → Better
Future Recommendations**

The record underneath it is:

**What the client wanted → who performed it → what was agreed → what was done →
what the result looked like → whether the client liked it**

This intelligence should assist people, not manufacture trust. It requires
privacy, consent, accurate data, and a demonstrated user benefit.

## Boundaries

- Do not call professional-submitted content verified.
- Do not promise identity, license, portfolio, or result verification before a
  defined process exists.
- Do not add AI merely because the data could support it.
- Do not build a full salon operating system without repeated demand.
- Do not let future vision distract from production reliability and local marketplace density.

## Validation Questions

1. Does structured proof of work improve a client's confidence?
2. Will professionals maintain richer records when the workflow is simple?
3. Which information prevents the most confusion before and after a service?
4. Can Lumina generate trustworthy reputation signals from real usage?
5. What part of this vision would users genuinely miss if Lumina disappeared?
