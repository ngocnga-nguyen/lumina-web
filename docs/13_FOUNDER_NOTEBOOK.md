## 🌱 Founder Philosophy

### 1. Businesses exist to create value.

Profit is not the purpose of a business. Profit is what allows a business to continue fulfilling its purpose.

**Reflection**

A mission without profit eventually disappears. Profit allows a company to continue serving customers, hiring great people, and improving its product.

---

## 💎 Mental Models

### Mental Model #1: People don’t buy products. People buy better versions of themselves.

**Examples**

- Nike → Confidence and performance
- Apple → Simplicity and identity
- Rhode → Healthy skin and confidence
- Lumina → Trust and confidence before the appointment

### Mental Model #2: Where there’s a hassle, there’s a business opportunity.

**Reflection**

Businesses get paid for removing:

- Confusion
- Uncertainty
- Effort
- Waiting
- Stress
- Inconvenience

**Lumina**

The biggest hassle is not searching. It is the fear of choosing the wrong beauty professional.

### Mental Model #3: People don’t pay for objective value. They pay for perceived value.

Perceived value increases when you:

- Satisfy human drives
- Remove hassle
- Create an easy-to-imagine outcome
- Build trust
- Create a premium experience

### Mental Model #4: Premium is not a price. Premium is a feeling.

Premium comes from:

- Thoughtful design
- Trust
- Consistency
- Simplicity
- Attention to detail
- Customer care

Premium does not necessarily mean high prices.

### Mental Model #5: Great businesses don’t just create products. They remove friction from people’s lives.

**Examples**

- Amazon → Shopping friction
- Uber → Transportation friction
- StockX → Trust friction
- Lumina → Beauty discovery and trust friction

- Why would someone choose Lumina over Instagram?
